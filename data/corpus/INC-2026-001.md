# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-001

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-CNC-07 §4 · **Effective:** 2026-03-01

## §1 Summary

RCA of two spindle-clamp bolt fatigue failures on CNC-07 concluded the manual torque of 45 N·m was insufficient for the HSK-A63 drawbar. Effective immediately the spindle clamp bolts must be torqued to 52 N·m.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spindle clamp bolts | 45 N·m | 52 N·m |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- Fracture surfaces showed high-cycle fatigue originating at the thread root.
- Engineering analysis shows 52 N·m keeps preload above the drawbar separation load.
- MANUAL-CNC-07 §4 remains accurate for all other fasteners.
