# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-002

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-PRS-02 §4 · **Effective:** 2026-02-18

## §1 Summary

To eliminate intermittent E-318 trips during rapid approach, the hydraulic relief pressure on PRS-02 is raised from 2500 PSI to 2750 PSI.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hydraulic relief pressure | 2500 PSI | 2750 PSI |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- The higher setting stays below the accumulator safety limit of 3000 PSI.
- Update the machine placard after the change; record the gauge serial.
