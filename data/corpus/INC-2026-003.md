# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-003

**Supersedes:** QC-WI-005 §3 · **Effective:** 2026-01-29

## §1 Summary

Customer PPAP feedback requires the hole-position true-position tolerance on the Cell 4 bracket to tighten from 0.05 mm to 0.03 mm.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hole-position tolerance | 0.05 mm | 0.03 mm |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- Applies to all Cell 4 brackets shipped after 2026-01-29.
- CMM-04 probe qualification interval is unchanged (see INC-2026-008).
