# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-004

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-WELD-06 §4 · **Effective:** 2026-02-05

## §1 Summary

Excess E-815 events traced to drive-roll slip. Drive-roll tension is raised from 8 N to 11 N.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wire-feed drive-roll tension | 8 N | 11 N |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- Higher tension eliminated slip on 1.2 mm wire in a 30-part trial.
- Re-check the liner for kinks if slip persists at 11 N.
