# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-005

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-LASER-02 §5 · **Effective:** 2026-03-11

## §1 Summary

Nitrogen assist-gas pressure is raised from 180 PSI to 210 PSI to improve dross-free cutting of 6 mm mild steel.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assist-gas (N2) pressure | 180 PSI | 210 PSI |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- Lower pressures produced adherent dross above 5 mm thickness.
- Verify with QC-WI-008 edge inspection after the change.
