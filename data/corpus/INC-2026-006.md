# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-006

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-CONV-05 §4 · **Effective:** 2026-01-15

## §1 Summary

Recurrent E-150 belt-tracking faults traced to under-tension. Belt tension is corrected from 45 N to 58 N.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Belt tension | 45 N | 58 N |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- The corrected tension holds the belt within 5 mm of centre over a full lap.
- Re-track the belt after any splice replacement.
