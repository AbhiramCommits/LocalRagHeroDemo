# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-007

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-GRND-03 §2 · **Effective:** 2026-02-27

## §1 Summary

Surface-finish drift (Ra above 0.4 µm) between dresses requires shortening the wheel-dressing interval from 40 h to 25 h of grinding.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wheel-dressing interval | 40 h | 25 h |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- The shorter interval keeps Ra inside the QC-WI-003 window.
- Log each dress on the grinder PM sheet.
