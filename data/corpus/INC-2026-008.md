# Northline Precision — Incident / Change Note INC-2026-008

**Supersedes:** MANUAL-CMM-04 §2 · **Effective:** 2026-03-20

## §1 Summary

Probe calibration interval is shortened from 90 days to 60 days after a drift event exceeded the 0.35 µm probe-repeatability budget.

## §2 Change Record

| Item | Previous value | New value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Probe calibration interval | 90 days | 60 days |

## §3 Engineering Notes

- CMM-04 must be re-qualified on the reference sphere every 60 days.
- Record the reference-sphere serial and the qualification result.
