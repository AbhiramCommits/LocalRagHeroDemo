# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-001

**Title:** Incoming raw-material verification (aluminium 6082-T6)  
**Asset:** ALL · **Inspection interval:** every incoming lot

## §1 Purpose

Verify mill certificates and hardness of aluminium 6082-T6 billets before release to Cell 4.

## §2 Procedure

1. Match the heat number on the billet to the mill certificate.
2. Measure hardness in three locations with the Equotip; record the mean.
3. Confirm the certificate is signed and traceable to EN 10204 3.1.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Hardness 95–115 HB and a valid 3.1 certificate on file.
- **FAIL:** Any reading outside 95–115 HB, or a certificate that does not match the heat number — quarantine the lot.

## §4 Tooling

Equotip 550, calibrated calipers
