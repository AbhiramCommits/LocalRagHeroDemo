# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-003

**Title:** Surface-finish (Ra) inspection  
**Asset:** GRND-03 · **Inspection interval:** every 20 parts

## §1 Purpose

Verify the ground surface meets the drawing Ra requirement.

## §2 Procedure

1. Clean the surface and take three Ra readings with the portable roughness tester.
2. Compare the mean against the drawing callout.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Mean Ra ≤ 0.4 µm and no single reading above 0.6 µm.
- **FAIL:** Ra above 0.4 µm — dress the wheel (GRND-03 §2) and re-measure.

## §4 Tooling

Mitutoyo SJ-210 roughness tester
