# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-004

**Title:** Thread pitch-diameter gauging  
**Asset:** CNC-12 · **Inspection interval:** every 50 parts

## §1 Purpose

Verify the pitch diameter of turned threads with calibrated gauges.

## §2 Procedure

1. Select the go/no-go gauge matching the thread callout.
2. The go gauge must enter by hand; the no-go must not enter more than two turns.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Go enters fully, no-go does not exceed two turns.
- **FAIL:** No-go enters more than two turns — rework or scrap the part.

## §4 Tooling

Calibrated go/no-go thread gauges
