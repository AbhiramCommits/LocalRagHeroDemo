# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-006

**Title:** Weld bead visual and penetrant inspection  
**Asset:** WELD-06 · **Inspection interval:** 100 % visual, 10 % dye-penetrant

## §1 Purpose

Verify weld quality on the Cell 4 structural bracket.

## §2 Procedure

1. Inspect the bead visually for undercut, porosity and leg length.
2. Apply dye penetrant to a 10 % sample and develop for 10 minutes.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** No cracks, leg length within ±0.5 mm, no penetrant indications.
- **FAIL:** Any crack or linear indication — quarantine and re-weld.

## §4 Tooling

Penetrant kit, weld gauges
