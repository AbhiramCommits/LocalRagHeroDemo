# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-007

**Title:** Torque verification of safety-critical fasteners  
**Asset:** ASM-01 · **Inspection interval:** every shift (driver verification), 5 % audit per shift

## §1 Purpose

Verify that safety-critical fasteners are torqued to specification.

## §2 Procedure

1. Run the shift-start verification on the transducer to 22 N·m.
2. Audit 5 % of finished assemblies by re-torquing to the spec.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Driver within ±5 % of 22 N·m and no audited joint loosening.
- **FAIL:** Driver out of tolerance — recalibrate before running production.

## §4 Tooling

Calibrated torque transducer
