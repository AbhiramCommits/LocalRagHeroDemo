# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-008

**Title:** Laser cut edge-quality (dross) inspection  
**Asset:** LASER-02 · **Inspection interval:** every 30 parts

## §1 Purpose

Verify cut edge quality on laser-cut blanks.

## §2 Procedure

1. Inspect the bottom edge for adherent dross.
2. Measure the kerf width with the optical comparator.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** No adherent dross and kerf width 0.20 mm ±0.03 mm.
- **FAIL:** Excessive dross or kerf out of range — adjust focus/gas and re-cut.

## §4 Tooling

Optical comparator
