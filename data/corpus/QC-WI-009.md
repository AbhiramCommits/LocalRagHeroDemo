# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-009

**Title:** Coolant concentration and pH check  
**Asset:** CNC-07 · **Inspection interval:** daily

## §1 Purpose

Keep the CNC coolant within its controlled window.

## §2 Procedure

1. Draw a sample and read concentration with the refractometer.
2. Measure pH with a calibrated meter.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Concentration 6–8 % and pH 8.5–9.5.
- **FAIL:** Outside the window — adjust concentration or dump and recharge.

## §4 Tooling

Refractometer, pH meter
