# Northline Precision — QA Work Instruction QC-WI-010

**Title:** Final dimensional audit and packaging  
**Asset:** ALL · **Inspection interval:** 100 % of shipped lots (sampled audit)

## §1 Purpose

Final release gate before parts leave Cell 4.

## §2 Procedure

1. Audit the lot against the CTQ list.
2. Confirm the FAI and non-conformance records are complete.
3. Apply the rust-preventive and package per the work order.

## §3 Pass / Fail Criteria

- **PASS:** Complete traceability records and conforming audit sample.
- **FAIL:** Any missing record or non-conformance — hold the lot.

## §4 Tooling

Calipers, CMM-04
